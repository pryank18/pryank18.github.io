Vendor onboarding in under half a day

A B2B vendor platform for petrol-pump owners at a fuel-delivery startup in India. The fix turned out to be a rulebook more than a feature, and it came from the pumps.

Role
Product Manager, FuelBuddy
Setting
Fuel-delivery startup, India
Type
B2B platform, 0→1
  • <½ dayonboarding, down from a full day plus a next-day review
  • 300+conversations with petrol-pump owners, at their pumps
  • 150+vendors live on the platform

Problem

Bringing a pump on as a vendor took a full working day of document collection and checks, then a second review the next day, so no new vendor could take orders the day they signed up.

Field evidence

300+ conversations with petrol-pump owners, at their pumps rather than over a survey. They showed which checks repeated for every vendor and could be standardised.

What I built

Standard validation and compliance rules inside the onboarding flow, so a submission was checked once, against one rulebook, at the moment of entry instead of the next morning.

Result

Onboarding came down to under half a day, for 150+ vendors now live on the platform.

What I'd keep

The fix was a rule set more than a feature, and it came from the pumps, not from a document. Every platform I've built since started the same way.
