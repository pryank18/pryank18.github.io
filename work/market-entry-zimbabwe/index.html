A new country, from zero to 8 depots and 50+ B2B clients

Choosing a market, launching B2B bulk fuel in Zimbabwe, and finding what the product has to be before it has to be software.

Role
Product Manager, then Country Head & Product Manager
Where
Harare, Zimbabwe
When
Apr 2025 – present

  • 8active depots in a country entered from zero
  • 50+enterprise and regional B2B clients in nine months
  • ~15suppliers live in month one

Problem

The company wanted to expand into Africa, with no local presence, suppliers, banking or customers in any of the candidate countries.

Evidence

Evaluated Zimbabwe, Zambia and Nigeria for the CEO, then picked Zimbabwe. On the ground, 40+ discovery interviews with transporters, vendors, fuel stations and corporates shaped what to sell first and what the product would need later.

Decision

The business before the product: banking, procurement, credit, dispatch and reporting under written SOPs, with ~15 suppliers live in month one and cross-border USD settlement with them. The interviews became FMS and VMS requirements fed through a live feedback loop.

Result

A live B2B business with 8 active depots, 50+ enterprise and regional B2B clients in nine months from a 400+ transporter and fleet network across six cities, and the Country Head role added to the product one.

  1. Before the move

    Evaluated Zimbabwe, Zambia and Nigeria for the CEO, and picked Zimbabwe.

  6. Now

On the ground

What I'd do differently

I scoped an on-site fuel management system for a large enterprise client, hardware and digital payments included, before confirming the hardware could be sourced in-country. It's paused. In a new market, check the supply chain for the product before you check the appetite for it.
