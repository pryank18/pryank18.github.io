Leadership

How I lead product

Today I run a country P&L and an eight-person team. Before that I was one of four PMs working with the CEO, CTO and a 15-engineer team.

Operating principles

  1. Go to the site before writing the spec. The best fuel products I've shipped started with a walk around a depot, not a survey form.
  2. Price on value you can prove. If the buyer can verify the saving, they'll share it.
  3. A metric without a baseline is a wish. Every goal gets a starting number and a date.
  4. Say what we won't build. A scope is only as good as its non-goals list.
  5. Teams own the how, leadership owns the why. My job is strategy, trade-offs and clearing the path.
  6. AI in the workflow, not on the slide. Agents compress discovery, prototyping and ops; measure the time saved like any feature.

How I'd approach the first 90 days in a new product role
0–30
ListenCustomer and site visits, data audit, P&L by product line, 1:1s with every product, engineering and sales lead. Output: a written diagnosis.
31–60
FocusPick the one or two bets that matter, write the kill/park list, set baselines. Output: one-page strategy and a scorecard the CEO and team sign.
61–90
